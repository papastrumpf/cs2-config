[h1]📥 Installation Guide[/h1]

[h2]Step 1: Download the Config from GitHub[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Open the GitHub page with the [b]🔗 View config here![/b] link at the top of this guide
[*]Click the green [b]<> Code[/b] button
[*]Click [b]Download ZIP[/b]
[*]Open your Downloads folder, right-click [b]cs2-config-main.zip[/b] → [b]Extract All…[/b] → [b]Extract[/b]
[/olist]

[h2]Step 2: Open Your CS2 cfg Folder[/h2]
[olist]
[*]In Steam, right-click [b]Counter-Strike 2[/b] → [b]Manage[/b] → [b]Browse local files[/b]
[*]Open [b]game[/b] → [b]csgo[/b] → [b]cfg[/b]
[/olist]
Default locations:
[code]
Windows: C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Counter-Strike Global Offensive\game\csgo\cfg
Linux: ~/.steam/steam/steamapps/common/Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg
[/code]

[h2]Step 3: Copy Files[/h2]
From the extracted [b]cs2-config-main[/b] folder, copy these into your [b]cfg[/b] folder (click [b]Replace[/b] if Windows asks):
[list]
[*][b]autoexec.cfg[/b]
[*]the [b]movement[/b] folder
[*]the [b]guide[/b] folder (prints the bind table on load)
[/list]

[h2]Step 4: Add Launch Options[/h2]
[olist]
[*]In Steam, right-click [b]Counter-Strike 2[/b] → [b]Properties[/b]
[*]On the [b]General[/b] tab, find [b]Launch Options[/b]
[*]Paste this:
[/olist]
[code]-novid -nojoy -fullscreen +fps_max 0 +engine_low_latency_sleep_after_client_tick true +exec autoexec.cfg[/code]

[h2]Step 5: Verify Installation[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Start CS2 and press [b]~[/b] to open the console (if it doesn't open: Settings → Game → Enable Developer Console → Yes)
[*]You should see the bind table and no "Unknown command" lines
[*]If not, type [b]exec autoexec[/b] in the console and press Enter
[/olist]

[h1]🚀 Null-Bind Movement[/h1]

[h2]How It Works[/h2]
[list]
[*]W/A/S/D use null binds: when you hold two opposite keys, the last one pressed wins
[*]Built on the analog [code]forwardback[/code] / [code]rightleft[/code] commands, one movement command per bind
[*]Loaded automatically by autoexec ([code]exec movement/setup[/code]), no sv_cheats needed
[/list]

[spoiler][b]⚠️ Note: Since August 2024 Valve says null binds and Snap Tap are not allowed and may kick for them on official servers. Use at your own risk in matchmaking, or remove the exec movement/setup line from autoexec.[/b][/spoiler]

[h2]Jumpthrow[/h2]
No script needed: CS2 gives every grenade released on the way up of a jump the same trajectory. SPACE is a plain jump.

[h1]🎯 Pro Tips[/h1]

[list]
[*][b]Clutch Situations[/b]: Hold SHIFT (walk) or CTRL (crouch) for louder footsteps, quieter teammate voices and a brighter screen for dark corners
[*][b]Quick Defuse[/b]: Hold E while defusing for a bigger radar, louder footsteps and quieter teammate voices
[*][b]Bomb Timing[/b]: The 10-second bomb warning is audible and the bomb music plays low, so you know when to defuse or save
[*][b]Silent Reload[/b]: Hold R to reload without making noise (slower), since the September 2026 update. No bind needed
[*][b]Fast Weapon Switch[/b]: Use Q for instant weapon cycling
[*][b]Bomb Drop[/b]: MOUSE3 drops the bomb, switches back to your weapon and tells your team "Bomb dropped"
[*][b]Enemy Mute[/b]: Press J to toggle enemy voice for focused play
[*][b]Spraying[/b]: Hold right + left click together when spraying (if it feels good) so the crosshair follows your spray. Tap with just left click and the crosshair stays static. Don't use it with pistols: you tap them, so there's no spray to follow, and right click toggles Glock burst and R8 alt fire. Silencers on the M4A1-S and USP-S never come off (the config disables detaching).
[*][b]Network Info[/b]: Ping, packet loss and frame time pop up on the HUD only when they get bad. Hold TAB to see them all plus the network quality graph
[*][b]Mic Check[/b]: Not sure your mic works? Press P, hold MOUSE5 and talk, and you hear yourself. Press P again to turn it off before the match
[*][b]Jumpthrow[/b]: No bind needed, CS2 gives every grenade released on the way up of a jump the same trajectory
[/list]
