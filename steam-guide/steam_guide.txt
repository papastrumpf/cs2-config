[h1]📥 Installation Guide[/h1]

[h2]Step 1: Download Configuration[/h2]
Download the configuration files from the GitHub repository and extract them.

[h2]Step 2: Locate Your CS2 Config Directory[/h2]
[code]
Windows: C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Counter-Strike Global Offensive\game\csgo\cfg
Linux: ~/.steam/steam/steamapps/common/Counter-Strike Global Offensive/game/csgo/cfg
[/code]

[h2]Step 3: Copy Files[/h2]
[list]
[*]Copy [b]autoexec.cfg[/b] to your cfg directory
[*]Copy the entire [b]movement[/b] folder to your cfg directory
[*]Copy the entire [b]guide[/b] folder to your cfg directory (prints the bind table on load)
[/list]

[h2]Step 4: Add Launch Options[/h2]
Right-click CS2 in Steam → Properties → Launch Options
[code]-novid -nojoy -fullscreen +fps_max 0 +engine_low_latency_sleep_after_client_tick true +exec autoexec.cfg[/code]

[h2]Step 5: Verify Installation[/h2]
Launch CS2 and open console (~). You should see the bind table and no "Unknown command" lines. If not, type [code]exec autoexec[/code].

[h1]⚡ Performance Optimizations[/h1]

[list]

[*]Maximum ping limit: 40ms

[h1]🚀 Null-Bind Movement[/h1]

[h2]How It Works[/h2]
[list]
[*]W/A/S/D use null binds: when you hold two opposite keys, the last one pressed wins
[*]Built on the analog [code]forwardback[/code] / [code]rightleft[/code] commands, one movement command per bind
[*]Loaded automatically by autoexec ([code]exec movement/setup[/code]), no sv_cheats needed
[/list]

[spoiler][b]⚠️ Note: Since August 2024 Valve says null binds and Snap Tap are not allowed and may kick for them on official servers. Use at your own risk in matchmaking, or remove the exec movement/setup line from autoexec.[/b][/spoiler]

[h2]Jumpthrow[/h2]
No script needed: CS2 gives every grenade released on the way up of a jump the same trajectory. SPACE is a plain jump.

[h1]🔧 Troubleshooting[/h1]

[h2]Config Not Loading[/h2]
[list]
[*]Verify files are in correct directory
[*]Check console for error messages
[*]Make sure launch options are set correctly
[*]Try manual execution: [code]exec autoexec[/code]
[/list]

[h2]Movement Issues[/h2]
[list]
[*]Check that the movement folder is properly copied
[*]Console should print "Loaded null movement aliases."
[*]Check binds with [code]bind w[/code] (should show +w)
[/list]

[h2]Grenade Keys Not Working After Reinstall[/h2]
[list]
[*]Run [code]exec autoexec[/code] once, it sets X/F/Z/V and 1-5 on load
[/list]

[h2]Buy Scripts Not Working[/h2]
[list]
[*]CAPSLOCK must be held (not toggled)
[*]Some weapons require specific team (CT/T)
[*]Check money availability
[*]Verify key bindings in console
[/list]

[h1]🎯 Pro Tips[/h1]

[list]
[*][b]Clutch Situations[/b]: Hold SHIFT to boost volume and hear footsteps better
[*][b]Quick Defuse[/b]: Hold E while defusing for zoom effect
[*][b]Fast Weapon Switch[/b]: Use Q for instant weapon cycling
[*][b]Bomb Drop[/b]: MOUSE3 for quick bomb drops to teammates
[*][b]Enemy Mute[/b]: Press J to toggle enemy voice for focused play
[*][b]Spraying[/b]: Hold right + left click together when spraying (if it feels good) so the crosshair follows your spray. Tap with just left click and the crosshair stays static.
[*][b]Network Info[/b]: Ping, packet loss and frame time pop up on the HUD only when they get bad
